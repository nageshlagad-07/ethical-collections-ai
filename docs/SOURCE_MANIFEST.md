# Source Manifest

## Data provenance
- **No external or public datasets were used.** All data is synthetic, generated by `collections_ai/generator.py` with seed `20261022`.
- No real customer or personal information is present. Names are generated by Faker (`en_IN`); e-mail addresses use the reserved `@example.invalid` domain; phone numbers use the fictional pattern `+000-NNNNNNNNNN`.
- Dataset files: `data/full/*.csv` (+ `ground_truth.csv|json`, `manifest.json` with per-file SHA-256 and the generator command); small development dataset reproducible with `python -m collections_ai.cli generate --loans 300 --out data/dev`.

## Reference material
- YottaFlex AI challenge documents supplied to the candidate (problem statement, Submission Guidelines, Candidate FAQ). They define the requirements; they are **not redistributed** in this repository because the invitation e-mail carries a confidentiality notice. Requirements are mapped in `docs/REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md`.
- Business rules are original and illustrative; no regulation text was copied and no legal requirement is asserted (see `docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md`).
